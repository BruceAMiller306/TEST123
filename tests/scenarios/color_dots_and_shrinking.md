# Color dots and shrinking

Given a green snake with length 5 and a green dot on the board
When any snake moves through the green dot
Then the green snake loses a square and its length becomes 4
And the green dot is consumed

Given a red snake with length 5 and an orange snake with length 5
When a snake moves through a red dot
Then the red snake loses a square
When a snake moves through an orange dot
Then the orange snake loses a square
